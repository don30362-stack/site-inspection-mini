heic-to embeds libheif and libde265 in its browser worker.
Upstream source: https://github.com/strukturag/libheif
Upstream source: https://github.com/strukturag/libde265

The decoder is imported dynamically as a separate Vite chunk only for HEIF content.
No heic-to, libheif, or libde265 source was modified for this project.
